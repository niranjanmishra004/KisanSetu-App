KisanSetu (Market Price Tracker) shows live Indian mandi prices backed by Agmarknet data.

- Live prices for 400+ crops with min/avg/max, market and arrival date
- State filter + search in English, Hindi and Bengali
- Price history charts (7 days to 1 year)
- Price alerts evaluated on-device with local notifications (no account, no tracking)
- Works offline with last-known prices; location stays on your device

No login, no signup. Alerts and location are stored only on your phone.
Live data: Agmarknet via data.gov.in. Source code is MIT licensed.
